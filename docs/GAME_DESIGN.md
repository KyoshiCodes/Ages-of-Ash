# Ages of Ash game design

The implemented Ember Ledger expansion is specified in [RETENTION.md](RETENTION.md): timed risk choices, capped offline reports, playable ledger consequences, crew memories, spatial supply, persistent faction pressure and four-hour Ash Cycles. It supersedes the original immediate job/level-30 ascension loop below at the HTTP boundary. Content and curve coefficients now live in validated JSON. A third starting-era operation makes sixty mastery reachable within the tested four-to-six-hour simulation.

The Ember Line is a current beneath history. Every era occupies one shared timeline: discovering a relic changes the operator's reach, not the past. A syndicate is a network of sworn operators; the player accumulates influence rather than conquering other players' permanent possessions.

## Four original eras

| Era                         | Places and allegiance                 | Discipline and conflict                                                                                                                                                               |
| --------------------------- | ------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Veiled Streets, level 1     | Cinder Quay; The Brass Vow            | Present-day counting houses, contraband routes and sworn neighborhood crews. The Ember Line appears in impossible patterns across ledgers.                                            |
| Tidebound Accord, level 5   | Sereglass Steps; The Stillwake Order  | Four martial domains: Saltwake (water), Flintpulse (earth), Breathweft (air), Cinderturn (fire). Monastic orders patrol the Pale Reflection, a spirit layer threatening the pilgrims. |
| Crownfall Reach, level 12   | Veyrwood March; The Thornward Compact | Mountain oath-forges, forest marches and the Ulven Fen. The Hollow Crown corrupts alliances; crews carry Thornward Oathiron to resist it.                                             |
| Far Ember Expanse, level 20 | Nacre Drift; The Heliox Assembly      | Fractured orbital authority, arcblade disciplines and smuggling lanes. The Quiet Meridian carries crews between capital fleets following a stellar manifestation of the Line.         |

The four eras are permanently represented in the world. Level gates control action access; advancement opens an era rather than creating a separate character. Ascension re-locks era actions until the required level is regained, while preserving equipment and crew.

## Mechanical specification

All times are server Unix milliseconds. `packages/gamedata/src/content.json` supplies content; `index.ts` supplies curves and named tuning constants. `packages/engine/src/index.ts` applies rules. Client previews are explanatory, never trusted for settlement.

## Economy and retention

Faucets: operations, timed holdings, boss victories, mission/login claims. Transfers: PvP and bounty escrow. Sinks: recruitment, equipment, crafting ingredients, upgrades, holding construction, upkeep, field tonics and influence. Crew price grows geometrically, while useful equipment also requires matching crew capacity. Hoarded holding income has a storage cap and raid risk. No loss of purchased gear from PvP.

First visit: name an operator, run four starter jobs, level up, allocate skills, buy a support item, recruit and build a holding. Ten-minute return: spend recovered energy and react to social messages. Hourly return: collect holdings and challenge a recovered solo boss. Daily return: missions, world contribution claims and login streak. Weekly return: complete weekly objectives and play Saturday Convergence. Seasonal return: compete for influence without deleting character progress.

## Thirty-day journey

| Days  | Intended journey                                                                                                       |
| ----- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1–3   | Learn four clocks, master Cinder Route, establish first holding, win a first Sentinel encounter, join an order.        |
| 4–7   | Reach Stillwake access, diversify affinity and loadout, participate in the first group boss, finish a weekly dispatch. |
| 8–14  | Reach Crownfall content, craft upgraded relic equipment, plan collection windows and a war declaration.                |
| 15–21 | Open Far Ember lanes, specialize attack/defense allocation, compete for region influence.                              |
| 22–30 | Work toward ascension, refine crew/gear efficiency, prepare next-season diplomacy.                                     |

These are tuning targets, not measured outcomes. Simulate session schedules before changing prices or gates; current eight operations are deliberately a compact content set.

Monetization policy: no paid power, gambling, randomized paid packs or artificial loss-recovery charges. Future sales may include explicit cosmetic bundles and a cosmetic-only season pass. Convenience cannot add combat capacity or resource throughput. V1 has no checkout and no real-money transactions.
